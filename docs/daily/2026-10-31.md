## Problems and troubleshooting

- **Problem:** The first accuracy pass matched predictions to ground
  truth pooled across every keyframe of a scene.
  - **Solution:** `micro_scores` matches inside each keyframe, then sums
    counts. The reported tables are from that second run.
- **Problem:** Unpinned 2 Hz overlap made BEVFusion slower than full-rate
  overlap (full-frame p95 808 ms versus 644 ms) because image decode
  still saturated all four vCPUs.
  - **Tried:** Pin MapTRv2 to CPU 0 and BEVFusion to CPUs 1–3.
  - **Solution:** p95 fell to 635 ms and the update rate stayed 1.994 Hz.
    The 500 ms target is still missed. Left as the measured result.
- **Problem:** Foxglove playback crashed on the first lane frame.
  `AttributeError: 'foxglove.messages.Point3' object has no attribute 'x'`,
  then `TypeError: must be real number, not NoneType` on an arrow whose
  endpoint had no elevation.
  - **Solution:** Labels use the numeric XYZ. Arrows with a missing
    endpoint height are omitted. Playback was restarted after the fix.

## Decisions

- Do not treat paper mAP, or the eight mini train scenes, as this
  checkout's accuracy.
- Do not invent a height for an empty elevation cell, and do not add the
  −1.8 m display offset on top of the grid.
- Keep MapTRv2 in Docker. The 2 Hz worker is a sidecar, not a second
  model inside the BEVFusion process.
- Report the pinned latency even though it misses 500 ms, rather than
  lowering the MapTRv2 rate or changing BEVFusion preprocessing to force
  the target.
