## Commands and code changes

- `projects/BEVFusion/demo/elevation_grid.py` — estimator. Seeds are points within 0.08 m of the cell minimum. A full plane is used only when those seeds span at least 0.4 m in both x and y and the normal is upright (`nz >= 0.85`). Otherwise the cell height is the median of the low returns, so a thin LiDAR ring cannot tilt the cell. Ground grows from cells within 0.30 m of the low-z hint, and a neighbor step steeper than `tan(25°) * 1 m` (0.47 m) is not crossed. Obstacle evidence counts points more than 0.10 m above the surface, plus a step between two flat cells.
- `projects/BEVFusion/demo/elevation_grid_demo.py` — synthetic check, 404-frame timing, PNG/NPZ, and MCAP.
- `projects/BEVFusion/demo/foxglove_stream.py` — keyframe grid packed as a Foxglove `Grid` (RGBA plus an `elevation` field) and logged with the rest of the scene.
- `.venv/bin/python projects/BEVFusion/demo/elevation_grid_demo.py` — completed. Synthetic ramp/curb/box check passed. 404 keyframes timed. Wrote the two PNGs, NPZs, `timing.md`, and `elevation.mcap`.

## Problems and troubleshooting

- **Problem:** Per-cell Python plane fits took about 78 ms on one keyframe.
  - **Solution:** Pack up to 32 low seeds per coarse cell and run one batched 3×3 covariance. Median ground fit on the 404 keyframes is 15.71 ms.
- **Problem:** Fitting a tilted plane through a thin LiDAR ring, then evaluating it at the cell center, painted concentric bands into the ground height.
  - **Solution:** Use that plane only when the seeds cover the cell. Otherwise store the median low-return height.
- **Problem:** The roof and other high patches stayed as ground because neighboring high cells agreed with each other.
  - **Solution:** Flood-fill ground from cells near the robust low-z hint. A step above 0.47 m is not crossed, so the roof stays off the surface and shows up as obstacle evidence.
- **Problem:** `foxglove.open_mcap` raised `FileExistsError: File exists (os error 17)` when `elevation.mcap` was already present.
  - **Solution:** Delete the existing file before writing it again.
- **Problem:** The Foxglove `Grid` Python object exposes `encode` and `get_schema`, not `column_count` or `data`.
  - **Solution:** The pack check calls `encode()` instead of reading those fields.

- **Problem:** Enabling `/elevation/ground`, `/elevation/slope`, and `/elevation/obstacle` in the already-open Foxglove page drew nothing.
  - **Tried:** Decoded a live `foxglove.Grid` message. The cell layout, pose `(-25, -25)`, 0.2 m cells, and RGBA plus `elevation` fields were valid. The playback server had already finished the 19 s scene and only published a topic when the frame index changed.
  - **Solution:** Stamp each grid with the keyframe time, because the 3D panel places an object by the timestamp inside the message. Republish the current frame every 0.5 s, including after the scene ends, so a topic enabled later still receives the grid. Restarted `foxglove_stream.py --play-scene` on `ws://127.0.0.1:8765`.

## Decisions

- No second network. The estimator is the Patchwork-style low-seed plane, stored as the survey’s layered elevation map.
- Height is measured from the local ground surface. A ramp stays in the ground and slope layers. A curb between two flat cells, and points more than 0.10 m above the surface, go to obstacle evidence.
- Detector time is not part of the grid timing. `--detections` on the demo can overlay BEVFusion boxes later; the pictures written today use nuScenes ground truth only.
- The live Foxglove scene still runs the detector. The elevation topics are computed from the keyframe cloud, not from the accumulated sweeps.
