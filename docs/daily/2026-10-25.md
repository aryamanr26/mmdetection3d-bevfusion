# 2026-10-25

## Elevation map on the road

The Foxglove heatmaps were drawing at the LiDAR origin, about 1.7 m above the road, because the grid pose was `z = 0` and the panel only applies the `elevation` field when that setting is selected.

`make_elevation_grids` now places the grid plane on the median ground height within 8 m of the vehicle. The `elevation` field is the rise of each bin above that road, in meters. Slope and obstacle layers use the same rise and sit 3 cm and 6 cm above it.

Playback was restarted on `ws://127.0.0.1:8765`. In the 3D panel, color mode is RGBA and Elevation field is `elevation`.
