# 2026-10-25

## Elevation map on the road

The Foxglove heatmaps were drawing at the LiDAR origin, about 1.7 m above the road, because the grid pose was `z = 0` and the panel only applies the `elevation` field when that setting is selected.

`make_elevation_grids` now places the grid plane on the median ground height within 8 m of the vehicle. The `elevation` field is the rise of each bin above that road, in meters. Slope and obstacle layers use the same rise and sit 3 cm and 6 cm above it.

Playback was restarted on `ws://127.0.0.1:8765`. In the 3D panel, color mode is RGBA and Elevation field is `elevation`.

## 3D multi-object tracking

Added a training-free, tracking-by-detection module on
`research/tracking`. BEVFusion detections are transformed from the LiDAR frame
to the nuScenes global frame, associated by class in two Hungarian stages, and
filtered with class-dependent CV/turn-rate Kalman models. Confirmed tracks
carry a stable ID, filtered velocity, speed, yaw rate, acceleration,
moving/static hysteresis, two seconds of history, and a three-second forecast.

`run_tracking.py` processed all 81 `mini_val` frames (5,409 low-threshold
detections and 3,909 confirmed/coasted outputs across two scenes). Official
nuScenes tracking evaluation produced 0.6640 AMOTA, 0.8084 m AMOTP, 0.6452
MOTA, 0.7027 recall, 15 identity switches, and 15 fragmentations. The
ground-truth identity oracle produced 0.9715 AMOTA and zero identity switches,
validating the transform, submission, and evaluator path.

Constant-velocity/turn-rate forecasts produced 0.5243 m ADE over the available
1/2/3-second checkpoints. FDE was 0.3626 m at 1 s, 0.5348 m at 2 s, and 0.7476
m at 3 s. These errors are conditioned on a predicted track matching current
ground truth within 2 m.

Foxglove playback now publishes `/tracks`: class-colored boxes, compact ID
labels, velocity lines, continuous history trails, and dashed predicted paths.
Class, speed, motion state, direction, yaw rate, acceleration, confidence, and
coasted state are inspectable entity metadata rather than always-visible text.
The 39-frame playback built and served all tracked frames successfully. Unit
tests passed for coordinate transforms, identity continuity/coasting, expiry,
and class-safe association.

## Tracking evaluation and baseline comparison

Evaluated the tracker on all ten nuScenes-mini scenes using fixed cached
BEVFusion detections. The comparison includes a new CenterPoint-style
constant-velocity baseline. The official devkit metrics were computed per scene
through custom splits, leaving `data/` unchanged. On the two val scenes, the
baseline reaches 0.808 AMOTA, compared with 0.664 for the Poly-MOT-style
tracker. The Poly-style tracker has fewer identity switches (15 vs 28) and less
velocity error on moving objects (0.46 vs 0.67 m/s). Single-factor diagnostics
trace most of the AMOTA gap to the 0.4 score required to start a track. With
births allowed from detections at 0.05, the Poly-style tracker reaches 0.790
AMOTA with 17 identity switches. Tracker defaults are unchanged.

Fixed per-log rather than per-scene tracker resets. The val result is
unaffected and reproduces at 0.6640 AMOTA. A yaw-rate update bug is documented
but not yet fixed.

Latency on the A10G: perception takes 339 ms mean and 358 ms p95, which is
within the 500 ms targets. The full Foxglove frame takes 529 ms mean and 567 ms
p95, which is over target because camera colorization takes 134 ms. Vectorized
association reduced `tracker.step` from about 50 ms to 5–7 ms with bit-identical
output. A GPU colorization prototype produced identical colors in 32 ms and is
the proposed next optimization. Foxglove playback was restarted afterward and is
serving 39 tracked frames on `ws://127.0.0.1:8765`.

Report: `AI_summary/2026-10-25-tracking-evaluation.md`.

## Track-start threshold and GPU colorization

`MultiObjectTracker` now has a separate track-start threshold, `birth_score`,
with a default of 0.05. The 0.4 score split between the two association stages
is unchanged. On the val scenes, AMOTA rose from 0.664 to 0.790 and AMOTP
improved from 0.81 m to 0.52 m, with 16 identity switches against 15 before.
AMOTA improves in every mini scene. The constant-velocity baseline still scores
0.808 AMOTA, but it makes 28 identity switches. `--birth-score 0.4` restores
the previous behavior.

`colorize_lidar` runs on CUDA when it is available. The colors are
bit-identical to the NumPy path, and colorization dropped from 134 ms to 34 ms.
The Kalman update now uses index slices, and `tracker.step` runs with
single-threaded BLAS. Together these keep tracking at 9.2 ms mean and 10.7 ms
p95, despite more tracks. The full Foxglove frame now takes 422 ms mean and
449 ms p95, and perception takes 337 ms mean and 357 ms p95. Playback was
restarted and serves 39 tracked frames on `ws://127.0.0.1:8765`.
