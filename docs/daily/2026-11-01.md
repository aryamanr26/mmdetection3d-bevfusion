## Problems and troubleshooting

- **Problem:** `import loaders` JIT-compiles RayIoU DVR from
  `lib/dvr/dvr.cpp` relative to cwd and fails under `/workspace`.
  - **Solution:** Register `loaders` as a namespace package and import
    only `loaders.pipelines`. DVR is not needed for inference.

## Decisions

- Keep SparseOcc in Docker. Do not put it in the BEVFusion `.venv`.
- Reuse MapTR or the repo CUDA 11.1 image would not have been faster:
  those are torch 1.9 / mmcv 1.4.
- Occupancy fusion is only an export plus Foxglove this pass. Tracking
  and the elevation grid are not joined yet.
